About me

I am a final year PhD student at Imperial College London, working on mathematical finance and supervised by Dr Eyal Neuman. My research interests include a variety of problems in market microstructure, studied with stochastic control and game theory among other methods. Previously, I completed my master's at the University of Warwick and my undergraduate studies at the London School of Economics.
